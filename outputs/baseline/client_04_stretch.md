## Recommendations

We recommend the following:

We recommend taking a proactive approach to your investments given the recent completion of the sale of James's business and your stated objectives of long-term growth with a balanced-to-moderate risk appetite.

Firstly, we suggest making full use of both ISA allowances for the new tax year. Contributing to the Stocks & Shares ISAs will enhance your tax efficiency and allow your investments to grow free from income and capital gains tax. 

In addition to the ISAs, we advise making pension contributions to both your SIPPs, up to the permissible limits. This will not only help increase your retirement savings but may also provide immediate tax relief, enhancing the overall growth potential of your investments.

Given the increase in the balance of the joint General Investment Account (GIA) on the Holloway platform, we propose a partial rebalance of this account. Disinvesting a portion of the GIA will help to optimise your investment strategy, ensuring that your portfolio remains aligned with your risk profile and investment goals.

Lastly, we recommend using the remaining balance from the business proceeds to establish a new jointly-held investment account. This account will offer you the flexibility to invest in a diversified portfolio that aligns with your long-term objectives, particularly in terms of growth and capital preservation.

Overall, these steps are designed to position your investments effectively to meet your future needs, including the aspiration to help with your grandchildren's school fees and any potential charitable donations down the line.
